\section*{Design and validation}
This experiment protects the pretrained $d2$ parents while exposing newly learned $d4$
routes in both blocks to the same regularization.  After parent pretraining, $E$ and the
rescaled base $V_1$ are frozen.  A small parallel trainable adapter $V'_1$ is added, and
phase 2 applies the same coefficient $\lambda$ to $V'_1$, $V_2$, and the $d4$ readout row
$W_4$.  The two $d2$ losses remain active.  As in the earlier accessibility sweeps,
$V_1\mapsto\alpha V_1$ controls the measured onset ratio $R_0=Q_2/Q_1$; the adapter starts
at $10^{-3}$ of standard initialization and negligibly perturbs $R_0$.

The completed grid contains eight $\alpha$ values, eight decay values, and three pretrained
seeds.  All $192$ result cells and all $192$ trajectory cells are present.  Unless stated
otherwise, numbers and phase colors below are three-seed means.  The red line marks the
theoretical frozen-feature threshold $R_c=1/\sqrt{8}=0.354$.

\section*{Main result: a compositional phase returns, but not exactly at $R_c$}
Matched adapter decay produces a clear blue-to-red boundary that was absent from the
matched-$V_2/W_4$ control.  At $\lambda=10^{-2}$, the mean block-2 $2{+}2$ fraction rises
from $0.010\pm0.004$ at $R_0=0.323$ to $0.340\pm0.119$ at $R_0=0.416$, then to
$0.880\pm0.015$ at $R_0=0.515$ and $0.899\pm0.019$ at $R_0=0.573$.  The same structure is
already visible at $\lambda=3\cdot10^{-3}$ ($0.028$, $0.318$, $0.848$, $0.888$ at those
four $R_0$ values).  Thus the theoretical threshold correctly identifies the side on which
composition becomes possible, but the empirical transition is broad and displaced toward
larger accessibility.  Its seed sensitivity is localized near the boundary: the largest
standard deviation is $0.119$ at $(\alpha,\lambda)=(1.5,10^{-2})$, whereas the high-$R_0$
phase has standard deviation at most $0.020$ for $\lambda\geq3\cdot10^{-3}$.

\section*{Comparison with the earlier controls}
The adapter intervention reverses the failure of the fully trainable controls.  At
$R_0=0.416,\lambda=10^{-2}$, the $2{+}2$ fraction is $0.340$ with the adapter, versus
$0.159$ under $V_2$-only decay and $0.0007$ under matched $V_2/W_4$ decay.  At
$R_0=0.515$ the comparison is $0.880$ versus $0.230$ and $0.0002$.  The frozen-parent
benchmark remains sharper: at nearby $R=0.438$ and $0.499$, it reaches $0.904$ and
$0.975$.  The adapter therefore recovers most of the frozen compositional solution at high
accessibility, while the onset cell remains mixed and variable.

This result identifies the missing condition in the earlier experiments.  Matching the
$V_2$ and readout penalties alone closes a scale escape but leaves the base representation
free to reorganize and bypass parent composition.  Freezing the pretrained parent-producing
map prevents that reorganization.  Under this constraint, matched regularization again
makes initial accessibility predictive of the block-2 circuit.

\section*{Route competition: block 2 composes, while block 1 remains direct}
The adapter's classified computation is $1{+}1{+}1{+}1$ in every cell
($f^{\rm adapter}_{2+2}<1.3\cdot10^{-33}$).  It is therefore the intended direct
first-block competitor, not another route to parent composition.  At $\lambda=10^{-2}$
and $R_0=0.416$, the mean signed contributions to $\hat f_4=0.986$ are $0.213$ from the
frozen base, $0.248$ from the adapter, and $0.526$ from block 2.  At $R_0=0.515$ they are
$0.252$, $0.103$, and $0.635$; at $R_0=0.573$, $0.336$, $0.042$, and $0.616$.

Thus increasing accessibility suppresses the penalized direct adapter and selects a
majority block-2 route whose internal computation is $88$--$90\%$ compositional.  It does
\emph{not} produce a pure end-to-end compositional phase: the unpenalized frozen base still
carries $25$--$34\%$ of $d4$ in the high-$R_0$ cells.  This fixed residual shortcut is now
the main obstruction, rather than a trainable first block learning around the penalty.

\section*{Accessibility drift and norm balance}
$R(t)$ changes much less than in the earlier fully trainable sweeps.  At
$\lambda=10^{-2}$, $R_0=0.323,0.416,0.515,0.573$ end at
$R_{\rm final}=0.341,0.429,0.534,0.578$.  Very low-$R_0$ cells rise more
($0.039\to0.167$), but no trajectory crosses from the clearly subcritical group into the
high-composition phase.  The circuit change is consequently associated with the controlled
initial representation rather than a common late-time attractor in $R$.

The matched penalty also avoids the catastrophic scale imbalance of $V_2$-only decay.  At
$\lambda=10^{-2}$, the norms $(V'_1,V_2,W_4)$ are $(1.041,1.200,1.643)$ for
$R_0=0.416$, $(0.543,1.124,1.404)$ for $R_0=0.515$, and $(0.266,0.901,1.145)$ for
$R_0=0.573$.  In particular,
$\lVert W_4\rVert/\lVert V_2\rVert=1.25$--$1.37$ in the transition and compositional
regime, compared with $3.22$ at $R_0=0.416$ in the matched-$V_2/W_4$ experiment and an
essentially collapsed $V_2$ under $V_2$-only decay.  The frozen benchmark is more nearly
balanced still (ratio about $1.08$ at nearby $R$), but the adapter control eliminates the
qualitative scale escape.

\textbf{Conclusion.}
Freezing the pretrained $d2$ parents and taxing the new direct and compositional routes on
equal footing restores the qualitative accessibility transition predicted by the frozen
theory.  The transition is not pinned sharply to $1/\sqrt8$: it occupies the interval
$R_0\simeq0.42$--$0.52$ and is most variable at its lower edge.  High accessibility selects
a robust, majority block-2 route with nearly pure $2{+}2$ internal structure.  A pure
network-level compositional phase is still prevented by the frozen first-block $d4$
shortcut, whose readout contribution remains substantial precisely where the adapter route
has been regularized away.
